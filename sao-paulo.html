Art & CultureMASP

MASP

Avenida Paulista

Lina Bo Bardi's red-beamed glass box floats above the avenue. Inside, paintings hang on glass easels in one open room — no walls, no prescribed route. The best collection of European art in the Southern Hemisphere.

2h visitClosed Mon
OutdoorsIbirapuera

Parque Ibirapuera

Vila Mariana

São Paulo's green heart: 400 acres of lakes, running paths and Niemeyer-designed pavilions. Come Sunday morning for the cyclists, the coconut vendors and the free museums clustered at its edge.

3h+ visitOpen daily

Sé & República

Start at Pátio do Colégio where the city began in 1554, then walk to the neo-Gothic Catedral da Sé, Theatro Municipal and the Art Deco Edifício Martinelli. Gritty, grand and entirely walkable by day.

República

Niemeyer's great concrete wave, 38 storeys and 1,160 apartments with its own postal code. Book ahead for the rooftop tour: the best 360° view of the endless grey horizon.

Liberdade

Japanese Quarter

Home to the largest Japanese diaspora on earth, marked by red suspended lanterns along Rua Galvão Bueno. Ramen shops, Okinawan bakeries and a Sunday street fair of takoyaki and yakisoba.

West Zone

SESC Pompéia

Água Branca

Lina Bo Bardi turned a drum factory into a cultural centre with two raw concrete towers joined by mid-air walkways. Theatre, pool, workshops and a canteen locals actually use. Free to wander.

Consolação

Know before you go

Getting around

The metro is clean, cheap and fast but doesn't reach everywhere. Buy a Bilhete Único card, and use ride apps late at night.

When to visit

April to September is drier and milder. Summer (Dec–Mar) brings heavy afternoon downpours that clear within the hour.

Eating

Lunch is the big meal — look for "prato do dia" specials. Japanese, Italian and Lebanese food here rivals anywhere.

Staying safe

Keep phones out of sight on the street, avoid Centro after dark, and carry only what you need for the day.
